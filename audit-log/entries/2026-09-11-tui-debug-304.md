## Design Decisions
- **Presentational, no hooks**: follows the inspector pane pattern (`#303`). No `useFocus`, no `useInput`, no Ink hooks — `isFocused` is a prop defaulting to `false`.
- **Reuses existing widgets**: `Tree` from `src/tui/widgets/tree/`, `Pane` from `src/tui/panes/primitives/base/pane-base`. No new primitives.
- **Type hierarchy**: `DebugBreakpoint` and `DebugFrame` extend `TreeNode` (file/line/enabled/hitCount fields); `DebugVariable` is a flat record (name/value/type) rendered by a local `VarRow` component.
- **Color tokens**: uses `colors.warn` (breakpoints), `colors.accent` (call stack), `colors.complete` (variables), `colors.fgDim` (labels/hints). No new color tokens.
- **Layout registration**: `debug` leaf added to `DEFAULT_LAYOUT` horizontal split at size 15, adjacent to `inspector` leaf. Constraint min 10 / max 50, resizable + collapsible — consistent with all other middle panes.
